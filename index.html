I made these cookies for my roommates last night. I started from the highest-rated recipe I could find, thinking if I added my own twist it could only get better. It did: one of my roommates said it was the best cookie she'd ever had in her life. Alice Waters says to be inspired by recipes, not ruled by them, so here's that recipe reviewed like code, with every change I made and why. No stars on this page.

Time is part of the recipe

I got the brown butter idea from an Instagram reel, but the reel said two hours and I didn't want to spend that long making cookies. So I took the idea and skipped the rest. Mine took about an hour: longer than the recipe card, half the reel. Waters says the "proper price" of food is more than money (Waters 7), and time is part of what I was paying. Skipping the slow version was a fast food choice, and I'd rather show that than hide it.
